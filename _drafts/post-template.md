**What I was looking at.** One or two sentences. Enough context that the finding
lands, no more.

**What I found.** The claim, stated once, plainly.

**How I know.** The evidence. Command, query, repro steps, or the source URL.
This is the part that separates a finding from an opinion.

**What I'm not claiming.** The limit of the result. Where it wasn't tested, what
would falsify it, what stays unexplained.

Aim for under 400 words. If it needs more than that it's probably two findings
or a longer piece.
