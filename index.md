Working notes and research index. Security engineering, mostly where machine
learning systems meet the rest of the stack.

[Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) is what I'm actively working, each
area anchored to something public. [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})
is upstream contributions and my own tools.
[Notes]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}) is short findings — what was tried, what
held up, what didn't. [Toolkit]({{ '/toolkit/' | relative_url }}) is the boring
but useful one: what I actually reach for during an investigation.

Current focus is adversarial evaluation of LLM and agentic systems. Prompt
injection that survives contact with a real pipeline rather than a bare model,
scoring harnesses that catch it, and what containment actually buys you once an
agent can reach the network.

Everything here is public work. Nothing from my employer, nothing under NDA, no
client material.
